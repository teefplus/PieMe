Privacy Policy — PieMe

Effective date: 10 November 2026
Publisher: Serhii Harntsarik · Contact: teefplus89@gmail.com

The short version

PieMe keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you enter, and nothing it reads from your calendar, is sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

What the app stores

On your device only:

  • The service types you create, with their prices and colours.
  • The activities you log or import, with titles, dates, durations, prices, client names, notes, and paid status.
  • Your settings (currency, time zone, week start, and similar).
  • A list of calendar events you chose to ignore.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your iPhone. It is removed if you delete the app, or if you use Settings → Delete all data.

Calendar access

If you choose to connect your calendar, the app asks iOS for access to your calendars. iOS offers no read-only option, so the permission is called "full access", but the app only reads events. It never creates, edits, or deletes anything in your calendar.

For the calendars you select, the app reads each event's title, notes, start and end time, which calendar it belongs to, and whether you declined it. It uses these to suggest which service an event belongs to and to log it as an activity. Events are read on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

You can turn calendar access off at any time in iOS Settings → PieMe → Calendars. Activities already imported stay in the app until you delete them.

Pro and purchases

The app is free to use, with an optional Pro upgrade (a yearly subscription) on iPhone. Buying, renewing, cancelling and restoring happen entirely through Apple and the App Store, using your Apple Account; Apple handles the payment and sends the receipt. The publisher does not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or purchase history, and the app has no server of its own that checks purchases: it asks the App Store on your phone and verifies Apple's signed answer there. Whether you have Pro is not stored in your data, and it is not part of a backup file.

If Pro ends, nothing you saved is locked or removed, and exporting and backing up stay free; only creating new Pro things stops. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What is shared

Nothing. The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and sends nothing to the publisher or anyone else. The only network traffic is the App Store's own, for showing prices and handling a purchase, which is between your phone and Apple.

The only way data leaves the app is when you ask it to: Settings → Export activities (CSV) and Settings → Back up all data open the iOS share sheet so you can send a file wherever you choose. That file is yours; the publisher never receives it.

Tracking and analytics

The app does not track you, does not use advertising identifiers, and collects no analytics or crash data of its own. (Apple may provide the publisher with anonymous crash and usage statistics if you have opted in to share them with developers in iOS Settings. This is controlled by iOS, not by the app.)

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

Because the publisher holds none of your data, there is nothing to request or remove from a server. You control your data directly: export it as CSV, back it up to a file and restore it, edit it, or delete it all from within the app.

PieMe on Android

Everything above applies to the Android app too, with these differences in wording:

  • The data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. It is removed if you uninstall the app, or if you use Settings → Delete all data.
  • The calendar permission is Android's Calendar permission (read only). The app declares no permission to change your calendar and no permission to use the network. You can turn the permission off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → PieMe → Permissions. Activities already imported stay in the app until you delete them.
  • The app is excluded from Android's backup and from phone-to-phone transfer on purpose, so your data never leaves the phone. When you change phones the new one starts empty. Use Settings → Back up all data first, keep the file somewhere you choose, and Restore from backup… on the new phone; the same file also works between an iPhone and an Android phone. (Export activities (CSV) is for spreadsheets and cannot be restored.)
  • Google Play may give the publisher anonymous crash and usage statistics if you have opted in to share them with developers. This is controlled by Android and Google Play, not by the app.
